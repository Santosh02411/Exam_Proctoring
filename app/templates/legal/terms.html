1. What this service is

This is an online exam-delivery and proctoring platform. Depending on your role, you may take proctored exams, or create/manage/review exams for an organization ("institution") using this service.

2. Accounts and organizations

Every account belongs to one organization (or, for a platform operator account, to none) — see the Privacy Policy for what that means for who can see your data. You're responsible for keeping your password confidential and for all activity under your account. Signing in from a new device or location may end a session you left open elsewhere; see the Privacy Policy's Security section.

3. Proctored exams

If you take a proctored exam through this service, you'll be asked to grant camera and microphone access, and to enroll a one-time reference photo before your first exam. During an exam, the service performs automated identity checks, monitors for signs of academic dishonesty (leaving the browser tab, exiting fullscreen, multiple faces in frame, unusual audio), and records the session. Declining camera/microphone access or enrollment means you won't be able to start a proctored exam. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what's collected, why, and for how long.

4. Academic integrity

Submitted answers may be automatically compared against other students' submissions to the same question for similarity, and flagged for an examiner's review. A flag is not, by itself, a finding of misconduct — it's a signal for a human to evaluate. Proctoring violations, similarity flags, and their review outcomes may be shared with your institution.

5. Acceptable use

Don't attempt to circumvent proctoring, impersonate another person, share exam content without authorization, or use the service to violate any applicable law or your institution's own academic-integrity policy.

6. Changes

These terms may be updated from time to time; the version number above changes when they do. Continued use of the service after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated terms.

7. Contact

For questions about these terms, contact your institution's administrator, or the operator of this platform.

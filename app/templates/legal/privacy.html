Privacy Policy

Version {{ version }}

1. Account information

Name, email address, phone number, and role. Your account belongs to one organization (an institution using this platform); that organization's own admins can see your account and activity within their organization. Organizations cannot see each other's data.

2. Biometric data (face descriptor)

Before taking a proctored exam, you enroll a one-time reference photo. The photo is processed in your browser to compute a 128-number mathematical descriptor of your face; the photo itself is never uploaded or stored — only that descriptor is sent to and kept by the server. During an exam, your live camera feed is compared to this stored descriptor (both in the browser and, if you upload a government/institutional ID, by the server independently) to help confirm the person taking the exam is the person who enrolled. This descriptor cannot be reversed back into a photo, but it is still biometric data and is treated as sensitive.

3. ID documents (if enabled)

If your institution enables enhanced identity verification, you may be asked to upload a photo of a government or institutional ID. The server runs OCR on it to extract identifying text and compares its photo to your live camera feed. The uploaded image is stored so it can be reviewed by an administrator if needed; a re-upload replaces the previous one.

4. Exam recordings and snapshots

During a proctored exam, your webcam and microphone are recorded in short chunks and uploaded for your institution's later review. Separately, periodic camera snapshots are analyzed automatically on the server; a snapshot image is only saved when that analysis flags something unusual (e.g., no face or multiple faces detected) — routine snapshots that don't trigger a flag are not kept.

5. Proctoring events and answer similarity

Events like leaving the exam tab, exiting fullscreen, or unusual audio are logged against your attempt and visible to your institution's admins/examiners/proctors. If you submit a descriptive or coding answer, it may be automatically compared to other students' answers to the same question for similarity, and a suspiciously similar pair may be flagged for an examiner's review.

6. Login and security data

Each login records the IP address and browser/device used, to support account security (for example, detecting a login from an unfamiliar device or location, and limiting an account to one active session at a time). This data is visible to your organization's admins and, in aggregate, to the platform operator.

7. How long data is kept

Exam recordings, snapshots, login history, and various activity logs are each kept for a configured retention period and then deleted automatically — by default, recordings and snapshots for 90 days, and login/security/activity logs for 180–365 days, though your organization or the platform operator may configure different windows. Your enrolled face descriptor and ID document are kept as long as your account remains active with identity verification enabled.

8. Who can see your data

Your own organization's admins, examiners, and proctors (as relevant to their role) — never another organization's. The platform operator does not have standing access to any organization's exam content, but may, for support purposes, use a logged, read-only, time-limited view of an organization's exam content at that organization's request.

9. Your choices

Proctored exams require camera/microphone access and face enrollment to start; declining means you can't take a proctored exam through this service. Contact your institution's administrator to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, subject to what your institution needs to retain for academic-integrity or record-keeping purposes.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact your institution's administrator, or the operator of this platform.
